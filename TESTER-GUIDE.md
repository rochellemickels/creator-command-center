# Creator Command Center — Friend Tester Guide

Thank you for helping Rochelle test the Creator Command Center. You do not need to be good with technology. In fact, honest feedback about anything confusing is especially helpful.

## Before you start

Open the public Creator Command Center link in the browser you normally use. Your information stays in that browser unless you download a backup. Rochelle cannot see your entries.

## Test the New Creator Setup

1. Choose **New Creator Setup**.
2. Enter your first name and brand name.
3. Choose your main platform, goal, niche, and preferred theme.
4. Select **Create My Command Center**.

## Try these tasks

- Add three priorities under **Today’s Big 3**.
- Save a thought in **Morning Brain Dump**.
- Add one future project and choose a platform for it.
- Add one content idea and change its status.
- Add a sample revenue entry.
- Complete part of the **Weekly Review**.
- Download a backup from the top of the dashboard.
- Open **Profile & Feedback** and prepare or copy your feedback.

## What Rochelle wants to learn

Please note:

- Anything you could not find without help.
- Any button or label that did not make sense.
- Anything that felt overwhelming or required too many steps.
- What felt easy, calm, useful, or encouraging.
- One feature you expected but did not see.
- Whether you would use this on a phone, tablet, or computer.

## Important privacy note

This first testing version does not use accounts or cloud storage. Information is stored only in the browser on the device where it was entered. Clearing browser data or switching devices can remove access to it, so use **Backup** before doing either.
