Friend testing guide

Try the Creator Command Center

You do not need to be “good with technology.” The purpose of this test is to see whether the website feels clear and easy for a first-time creator.

Your entries stay only in your browser. You will not see Rochelle’s private entries, and she will not see yours unless you copy and send your feedback.

Five simple steps

  1. Open the main website and choose New Creator Setup.
  2. Enter your name, brand name, main platform and one goal. Choose the color theme you like.
  3. Add three priorities on the Dashboard and save one thought in Morning Brain Dump.
  4. Add one Future Project and one Content Idea. Try moving the idea to its next stage.
  5. Open Profile & Feedback. Describe anything that was easy, confusing or missing, then use Copy Feedback to Share.

What Rochelle wants to learn

There are no wrong answers. Please notice where you pause, what words are unclear, which buttons you expect to work differently, and whether you know what to do next without instructions.

Important: Use the Backup button before clearing browser history or switching devices. Information saved locally does not automatically sync to another phone or computer.
